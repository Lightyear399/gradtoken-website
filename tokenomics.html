Why the largest share goes to the community

The largest single allocation, at 35%, goes to Community & Ecosystem Rewards — funding GradLearn and GradStake rewards directly — rather than to the team or early investors. GradToken's core pitch is utility-driven demand, not speculation, and the allocation is built to reflect that: the largest share goes to the people actually using the platform.

For scale, 1 billion GRAD sits between two comparable learn-to-earn tokens: BitDegree (BDG) has a max supply of 660 million, while STEPN (GMT), the most successful X-to-earn token to date, has a max supply of 6 billion. It's a normal, defensible number for this category rather than an inflated one.

Vesting: what actually unlocks, and when

Total supply size alone doesn't determine whether a token's distribution is aggressive — how much can hit the market at once does. Team, investor, and advisor allocations are all subject to a cliff followed by linear monthly vesting; none unlock in a lump sum.

  • — Team: 12-month cliff, then linear release over 24 months
  • — Investors: 6-month cliff, then linear release over 18 months
  • — Advisors: 12-month cliff, then linear release over 18 months

Community & Ecosystem tokens carry no calendar-based unlock at all — they are released only as earned or claimed through actual GradLearn and GradStake activity. All three vesting schedules are enforced on-chain by separate, independently verified GradVesting contracts — see the Security page for direct links.

Left deliberately open, in the interest of not presenting false precision: the exact Token Generation Event date (set once Phase 1 and Phase 2 are complete), the final investor allocation (currently reserved at 10%, could shift toward 0% if no funding round happens before TGE), and treasury governance structure (multisig now, DAO-governed under Phase 5).
