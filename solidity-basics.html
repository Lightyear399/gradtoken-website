Deploy & verify a contract

This is pass/fail, not scored. Write your own contract — not a copy of the lesson examples — deploy it to Sepolia, and verify it on Etherscan.

1. Pick one to build

A simplified token, a counter or voting contract, or a basic escrow/piggy-bank contract. Keep it small and yours.

2. What passes

Contract deployed to Sepolia and verified on Etherscan, isn't an unmodified lesson example, and your write-up correctly describes what it does plus one design decision you made.

3. Submit

Connected wallet submissions go straight to review, with contract verification checked automatically. Prefer not to connect a wallet? Fill in the form above and it'll open your email client to victorichies20@gmail.com instead.

Anchored to your actual work, not a quiz score.

Once reviewed, your GradLearn certificate for this course will reference your verified contract directly — anyone checking it can click through and see the real deployed code. On-chain issuance is coming soon; reviewed submissions will be queued for the first issuance batch.
