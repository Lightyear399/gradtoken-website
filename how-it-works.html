The core loop

Two products. One reason to come back.

GradToken isn't promising five products at once. Everything here is scoped to what's actually buildable by a small, focused team — starting with GradLearn and GradStake.

Learn

GradLearn

Course and quiz content with a clear earn-on-completion mechanic. Finish a milestone, receive GRAD — plus a blockchain-verified certificate that can't be faked or quietly revised.

  • Pay for courses in GRAD
  • Earn GRAD for completed milestones
  • Certificates issued on-chain, tamper-resistant
Earn

GradStake

Stake the GRAD you've earned and put it to work instead of letting it sit idle. Built with a minimum stake amount and hold duration — a deliberate anti-sniping design decided during penetration testing, not an afterthought.

  • Stake GRAD, earn GRAD rewards over time
  • Emergency withdraw always available, principal never trapped
  • Reward accounting battle-tested against real attack attempts

How GradLearn actually works

A learner's path through GradLearn is a straightforward loop, designed to give both education access and credential trust in the same product:

  1. Enroll in a course using GRAD. Course pricing is set in GRAD, keeping the whole loop inside the ecosystem rather than requiring a separate payment rail.
  2. Complete milestones and quizzes. Progress is tracked per-milestone, not just at course completion, so partial progress still counts toward earning.
  3. Earn GRAD back for verified completion. Completing a milestone triggers a GRAD reward — the "earn" half of learn-to-earn.
  4. Receive an on-chain certificate. The completed credential is issued on-chain, making it independently verifiable by any employer or platform — not just a PDF someone could photoshop.

How GradStake actually works

GradStake exists so GRAD earned through GradLearn has somewhere productive to go, rather than sitting idle in a wallet:

  1. Stake GRAD into the contract. A minimum stake amount applies — a deliberate anti-sniping measure, not a UX accident (see Security for why).
  2. Earn rewards proportional to your share of the pool, over time. The longer and larger your stake relative to the total pool, the more of the reward emission you earn.
  3. Claim rewards once the minimum hold duration passes. This isn't arbitrary friction — it's what closes a real reward-manipulation exploit found during penetration testing.
  4. Withdraw principal any time. An emergency withdrawal path is always available, instantly, even mid-lock — it forfeits pending rewards, but your principal is never trapped.

Why this pairing, specifically: GradLearn drives new user acquisition with a clean, low-regulatory-risk pitch — learn, and earn GRAD for it. GradStake gives those earners a reason to hold and use GRAD, closing the utility loop that gives the token real, non-speculative demand. Both are buildable by a small team without touching banking licenses or a two-sided marketplace's cold-start problem — unlike several ideas in GradToken's longer-term vision.
